# Working with content creators

Many Roblox breakouts were amplified by video creators: analyses of Dead Rails describe large Roblox YouTubers posting within days of Roblox's own social posts, then the game climbing the charts (GameAnalytics blog, 2026); Roblox's 2026 Halloween event, The Hollow, scheduled creator livestreams inside the event every day (Roblox newsroom, 2026-10-21); and the Innovation Awards include a Best Video Star category. This note covers how the studio works with creators without crossing money, honesty or safety lines. Community operations are in [Community building](../10-retention-and-social/community-building.md).

## Why creators matter

- **Distribution:** a video can bring more new players in a day than weeks of organic discovery.
- **Social proof:** players trust creators they watch; a game they play looks like the place to be.
- **Clips:** creators find and amplify the moments players share ([Clip moments and shareability](../02-core-loop/clip-moments-and-shareability.md)).
- **Feedback:** creators are expert players who spot problems early.

## What to give creators (no money involved)

| Offer | Notes |
|---|---|
| Early, accurate patch notes | A day ahead, embargoed until update time ([Anime community, updates and codes](../20-anime-games/anime-community-updates-and-codes.md)) |
| Private test servers | For recording footage before updates; no economy advantages carried into live servers |
| A creator kit | Logos, key art, short description, update calendar, contact |
| Clip-friendly features | Photo mode, replays, spectate, big reveals ([Viral and social mechanics](../21-hits-and-trends/viral-and-social-mechanics.md)) |
| Share links per creator | Track which channels bring engaged players ([User acquisition](user-acquisition.md)) |
| Credit | Thank creators in patch notes or the game's social posts, with permission |

## Fairness and honesty rules

1. **No gameplay advantages for creators** in public servers; anything given must be cosmetic or limited to test servers.
2. **Paid promotion is a money decision** for the human; it is escalated with `--kind money` and never agreed by an agent.
3. **Disclosure:** creators who are paid or given items must disclose it under the rules of the platform they post on; the studio asks for it in writing.
4. **Honest content:** no staged "secret" exploits, fake odds or misleading claims in sponsored videos; misleading marketing hurts retention and can break guidelines ([Thumbnails](../11-store-page-and-discovery/thumbnails.md)).
5. **Safety:** creators' audiences are young. Avoid content that encourages spending pressure, scams or off-platform contact; social links follow Roblox's rules ([Platform shifts 2025-2026](../21-hits-and-trends/platform-shifts-2025-2026.md)).

## Codes and creator moments

- A creator-specific **code** (modest, expiring) lets viewers get a small reward and lets the studio count redemptions; codes follow the same rules as any code ([Anime community, updates and codes](../20-anime-games/anime-community-updates-and-codes.md)).
- Live **admin sessions** scheduled with creators can produce huge peaks; plan them like events with fairness rules ([Live events and admin events](../21-hits-and-trends/live-events-and-admin-events.md)).
- Platform events run by Roblox may include creator livestreams; participation is through Roblox's programs, and any agreement is the human's decision.

## Measuring the effect

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| New players by channel | Share link acquisition analytics |
| Their D1 and D7 retention | Compare with organic players; a spike that does not retain is a cost, not a win |
| Code redemptions | Per-creator code counts (analytics event) |
| Concurrent players during and after a video or stream | Chart tracker snapshots before and after ([Reading the charts](../15-market-research/reading-the-charts.md)) |

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A creator shows an exploit to millions | Fix fast; communicate; never reward exploit content |
| Influx overwhelms servers or the first session | Load-test; make the first minute bulletproof ([First session blueprint](../01-first-five-minutes/first-session-blueprint.md)) |
| Expectations set by a video do not match the game | Give accurate information; keep thumbnails and videos honest |
| Dependence on one creator | Many smaller relationships; strong organic retention |

## For agents

- [ ] Creator offers are cosmetic, informational or test-server only; no live advantages.
- [ ] Any paid promotion or contract is escalated as a money decision.
- [ ] Each creator relationship has a share link and a code to measure impact.
