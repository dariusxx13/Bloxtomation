# User acquisition

User acquisition (UA) means getting new players into your game. On Roblox, the biggest channel is free: Recommended for You. Every other channel exists mainly to start or accelerate that engine, or to bring players the algorithm would never reach.

## The channels

| Channel | Cost | Strength | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recommended for You (organic) | Free | Largest scale | Earned through engagement signals; see [Discovery algorithm](../11-store-page-and-discovery/discovery-algorithm.md) |
| Sponsored ads (Ads Manager) | Paid | Instant, targetable | Does not change RFY ranking; can trigger exploration |
| Search ads | Paid | High intent | Exact keyword matches; up to 10 keywords per ad set; 13+ audience |
| Share links (off-platform) | Free | Trackable, Creator Rewards | TikTok, YouTube, Discord, X |
| Creators and influencers | Free to expensive | Huge spikes | Early access, codes, exclusive items |
| Friend invites and referrals | Free | High quality players | Rewards for both sides |
| Notifications and events | Free | Returning players | Opt-in players; event RSVPs |
| Charts and curation | Free | Momentum | Trending sorts; Standout Games for novel games |
| Search (organic) | Free | Intent | Good title, description, genre |

## Ads Manager essentials (platform facts, 2026)

- Run campaigns for your game with daily or lifetime budgets, auto-bidding, up to 10 creatives (16:9; AI-generated creatives available), shown on Home and in search.
- **Objectives**: an Earnings objective (limited availability) finds players likely to spend; an Engagement objective finds age-checked highly engaged players whose plays count toward the Kids and Select evaluation threshold (higher cost per play). Plays-focused campaigns drive volume.
- **Audiences**: new players (never played, or not in 180+ days); recent players (played in last 30 days; needs 10,000+ recent players); lapsed players (30-180 days; needs 20,000+ lapsed players). Advanced targeting by location, age, gender, genre and device.
- **Advanced join options**: send ad clicks to a specific place and attach launch data, so you can tailor onboarding ("welcome back" for lapsed players, a small ad-only reward for new players). Launch data is visible in the URL, so do not treat it as secret.
- **Moderation** takes up to about 24 hours; the first 24 hours of a campaign are a "learning" period.
- **Reporting**: impressions, clicks, plays, cost per play, playtime, earnings (including Creator Rewards) and ROAS for earnings campaigns. Attribution windows: new users' plays and spend are credited for 30 days; recent users only for the ad session; users returning after 7-29 days for 7 days; after 30+ days for 30 days. Data can take up to 48 hours.
- **Non-unique games may get no spend and no conversions**: ads cannot rescue a clone.
- Pay by card (18+) or by converting Robux to ad credits (13+, irreversible).

### Running ads well

1. **Fix the first session before spending.** Ads magnify your funnel. If ads bring players who bounce, you paid to learn what the funnel already told you. Roblox's own advice: if a campaign shows no progress after 3-5 days, improve onboarding before continuing.
2. **Test creatives.** Use several thumbnails; the winners inform your organic thumbnails.
3. **Small budget, measure, scale.** Start with a modest daily budget for a few days, compare cost per play with the earnings and retention of ad players.
4. **Use ads to kick-start exploration** after big updates, not as permanent life support.
5. **Compute payback**: estimated lifetime earnings per ad-acquired player (from attributed earnings plus retention) versus cost per play. Remember your DevEx conversion when comparing Robux earnings with dollars spent.

## Share links and off-platform growth

- Create a share link per channel and per creator in Creator Hub. They are trackable (clicks, signups, reactivations, spenders) and can carry custom launch data for perks.
- New users and reactivated users (60+ days inactive on Roblox) who join through your share links, play 10+ minutes that day, and stay attributed can earn you 35% of their first $100 of spending through Creator Rewards (with conditions). See [Platform payouts](../09-monetization/platform-payouts.md).
- Post short vertical clips of your game's clip moments (see [Clip moments and shareability](../02-core-loop/clip-moments-and-shareability.md)).

## Creators

- Identify creators who play your genre. Offer early access before updates, a code for their audience, an exclusive cosmetic, and a short brief with what is new.
- Give each creator a unique share link and code to measure their impact.
- Time creator content with your update releases.

## Measuring acquisition

- Creator Analytics > Acquisition: plays and new users by source (Home recommendations, search, sponsored, friends, external, and more).
- Share Links tab for off-platform links.
- Ads Manager reporting with attribution.
- Retention and spend **by source**: a source that brings many players who leave immediately is worse than a small source of loyal players.

## For agents

- [ ] Onboarding funnel and D1 are healthy before recommending paid UA.
- [ ] Every channel has its own share link or code for attribution.
- [ ] Ad campaigns start small with defined success criteria (cost per play, D1 of ad players, attributed earnings) and a stop rule.
- [ ] Ad spend proposals go to the human for approval with expected payback.
- [ ] Launch data used to tailor the first session for ad and invite players.
