# Grow and farm playbook

Farming games turn waiting into anticipation: plant seeds, watch them grow, harvest, sell, buy better seeds, expand. The genre produced one of 2025's largest Roblox hits, which combined an active, collaborative farm loop with frequent updates, events and weather-driven mutations.

Roblox genre: Simulation (often Idle or Incremental), sometimes Roleplay & Avatar Sim.

## Audience

Very broad, including younger players and players who prefer calm, social games. Strong daily return behavior.

## The loop

```
Moment:  plant / water / harvest -> crop pops with size and value
Session: sell crops -> buy seeds (rarer, more valuable) -> expand plot -> gear (sprinklers, tools)
Long:    rare seeds and mutations, pets that help, events, trading, decoration and status
```

## Core mechanics

- **Plots**: each player has a garden plot around a shared area (shop, market, event stage).
- **Seeds with rarity and growth times**: short (minutes) to long (hours); rarer seeds take longer and are worth more.
- **Stock shop rotation**: the seed shop restocks on a timer with random availability of rare seeds. Players gather for restocks.
- **Mutations**: crops randomly gain modifiers (from weather events, pets or items) that multiply value and look different.
- **Weather events**: rain, storms, special skies that trigger mutations and give everyone a shared moment.
- **Harvest and sell**: sell at a market with visible prices, or keep rare crops for collection or trading.
- **Gear**: tools that speed growth, protect crops, or boost mutation chances.

## Pacing

- First seed planted within 30 seconds; first harvest within about a minute.
- Early seeds grow in seconds to minutes; rarer seeds in tens of minutes to hours.
- At least one long timer that finishes the next day (return hook).
- Offline growth continues (crops finish while away), capped by plot capacity.

## Monetization

Rare seed packs (if random, disclose odds), restock skips or extra stock (careful: do not sell skipping the fun), gear, plot expansions, pets that help, cosmetic decorations, x2 sell price pass. Server-wide weather triggers are a social purchase players love. See [Product catalog](../09-monetization/product-catalog.md).

## Retention

Restock timers, weather schedules, crops finishing overnight, weekly seed and event updates, limited event seeds and mutations, collection index of crops and mutations, daily rewards.

## Social

Visiting gardens, gifting crops, trading rare seeds and pets, server-wide weather events, cooperative events (community harvest goals). Shared waiting time is social time.

## Clip moments

Huge mutated crops, rare seeds appearing in the shop, weather events transforming the garden, record sale prices.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Too much waiting, nothing to do | Short active tasks (watering, pests, mini-games) between harvests |
| Economy inflation from mutations | Caps, new sinks (decor, gear), new seed tiers |
| Restock exploit or desync between servers | Server-time-based restocks; consistent across servers if global |
| Trading scams and dupes | See [Trading economies](../04-progression-and-economy/trading-economies.md) |
| Selling skip-the-wait for core timers | Sell capacity and speed boosts with care |
