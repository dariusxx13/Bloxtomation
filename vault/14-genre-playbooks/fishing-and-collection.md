# Fishing and collection playbook

Fishing games became a major Roblox category in 2024-2026: cast, wait for a bite, win a short skill mini-game, reveal the catch with its size and rarity, sell or keep, upgrade the rod, travel to new waters. The loop is relaxing and suspenseful at once, and the collection is endless. The same structure works for bug catching, treasure digging, creature taming and mining.

Roblox genre: Simulation (or Adventure, Exploration), depending on emphasis.

## Audience

Broad: younger collectors and older players who enjoy chill progression. Long sessions with auto-fishing.

## The loop

```
Moment:  cast -> wait (anticipation) -> bite -> reel mini-game -> reveal (species, size, rarity, mutation)
Session: sell or keep -> buy rods, bait, upgrades -> travel to new locations -> rarer catches
Long:    journal completion, giant and mythic catches, events, weather and time conditions, trading
```

## Core mechanics

- **Reel mini-game**: a short timing or tension bar game that is easy to learn and harder for rare fish. It adds skill and keeps the loop active. Make it touch-friendly.
- **Rarity and size**: each catch has a species rarity and a random size (weight); size is a big part of the excitement and the bragging.
- **Locations**: each area has its own species list, conditions and look.
- **Conditions**: time of day, weather, seasons and events change which fish appear (a reason to return at different times).
- **Rods and bait**: rods improve luck, reel control and catch speed; bait targets certain rarities. Soft-currency progression with premium options.
- **Journal**: the collection index with silhouettes and hints about where and when each fish appears.
- **Mutations and variants**: shiny, albino, giant, event mutations.

## Pacing

- First catch within 20-30 seconds of spawning.
- First rare catch within the first session (seed a guaranteed early rare).
- New location reachable within 10-20 minutes.
- Auto-fishing as a later upgrade or pass, slightly weaker than active play.

## Monetization

Luck boosts (disclose effect on odds if paid), premium bait (paid random outcomes are regulated), better rods (sidegrade or fair progression), auto-fish pass, inventory space, boats and cosmetics, server-wide luck events.

## Retention

Weather and time rotations, daily quests ("catch 5 night fish"), weekly new species and locations, events with limited fish, journal completion rewards, leaderboards for biggest catches.

## Social

Fishing together (friend luck bonus), showing catches, server announcements for mythic catches, trading, tournaments (biggest fish in an hour).

## Clip moments

A mythic catch after a tense reel, record-size fish, rare weather events.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Waiting with nothing to do | Short waits, mini-game, ambient activity |
| Mini-game frustrating on touch | Generous timing windows on mobile; test on phones |
| Journal too big, no direction | Sets per location, hints, completion rewards |
| Copying a hit's name and look | Distinct theme and twist; discovery demotes non-unique games |
