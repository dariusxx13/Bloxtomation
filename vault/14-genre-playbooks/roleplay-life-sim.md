# Roleplay and life sim playbook

Roleplay (RP) games give players a world and tools to invent their own stories: a town with houses, jobs, schools, hospitals, vehicles and pets. They are among Roblox's most-visited games ever. There is little "goal" in the classic sense; the game is a stage for social play and self-expression.

Roblox genre: Roleplay & Avatar Sim (subgenres Life, Morph Roleplay, Animal Sim, Pet Care, Dress Up).

## Audience

Young players (many under 13), strongly social, high share of mobile. Long sessions (20-45 minutes).

## What players come for

- Expressing themselves (avatars, outfits, houses).
- Playing out stories with friends (family RP, school RP, police and robbers).
- Hanging out in a living world.

## Core systems

- **Town map** with recognizable places: homes, school, hospital, police station, shops, park, beach, secret areas. Each place supports roleplay with props and interactions.
- **Homes**: claimable houses with customization (furniture, colors). Saving home designs is a major retention feature.
- **Jobs and roles**: role tools (police, doctor, chef) with simple interactions.
- **Vehicles**: cars, bikes, boats; customization.
- **Pets**: adoptable, customizable companions.
- **Avatar tools**: outfit changing, accessories, name and bio tags.
- **Interactions**: sit, sleep, eat, carry, emotes, doors, phones.
- **Secrets and easter eggs**: hidden rooms and items give exploration goals and community content.

## Social tools

- Name tags with roleplay titles.
- Private houses with permissions (lock doors, invite friends).
- Emotes and animations for storytelling.
- Text that players write (names, bios, signs) **must** be filtered with `TextService`.

## Monetization

Premium houses, furniture packs, vehicles, pets, job perks, outfit items, VIP areas (cosmetic), name colors. In roleplay, expression is the product: monetize style and variety, not power.

## Retention

Saved homes and collections, weekly furniture or vehicle drops, seasonal town decorations and events, new places in town, pet care routines.

## Safety

Roleplay games attract young players and social interactions, so safety is central:

- Respect chat and age-tier rules; the game must work without chat for younger tiers.
- Do not build features that allow sharing contact details or moving off-platform.
- Moderate player-created content (names, house signs) through filtering.
- Avoid content that would push the rating up unintentionally (romance themes, violence). The questionnaire covers social hangout and free-form creation; answer accurately.
- Provide easy ways to leave unwanted interactions (block, private spaces).

## Clip moments

Funny stories, house tours, secret areas, chaotic vehicle moments.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Empty world feeling | Dense, interactive map; NPCs; ambient life |
| Too many systems at once for young players | Simple tools, icons, progressive reveal |
| Unfiltered player text | Filter everything others can see |
| Monetizing power | Monetize expression and variety |
