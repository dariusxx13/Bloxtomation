# 14 Genre playbooks

Each playbook covers audience, the loop, first session or core mechanics, systems, monetization, retention, social, clip moments and traps.

- [Simulator and incremental playbook](simulator-incremental.md): click, upgrade, zones, pets, rebirths.
- [Tycoon playbook](tycoon.md): plots, buy pads, droppers, build-out pacing.
- [Obby and platformer playbook](obby-and-platformer.md): stage design, difficulty curves, checkpoints, variants.
- [RNG and aura playbook](rng-and-aura.md): "1 in X" rolls, luck, auras, auto-roll, biomes.
- [Steal and defend playbook](steal-and-defend.md): bases, stealing, defenses, newcomer protection.
- [Grow and farm playbook](grow-and-farm.md): planting, restocks, mutations, weather, timers.
- [Tower defense playbook](tower-defense.md): waves, units, co-op matches, meta-progression.
- [Anime battlegrounds and fighting playbook](anime-battlegrounds.md): combat feel, movesets, hit validation, IP line.
- [Survival and horror co-op playbook](survival-horror-coop.md): tension pacing, monsters, cooperation, age ratings.
- [Roleplay and life sim playbook](roleplay-life-sim.md): towns, homes, jobs, expression, safety.
- [Shooters and PvP playbook](shooters-and-pvp.md): gunplay, netcode, matchmaking, cross-input fairness.
- [Fishing and collection playbook](fishing-and-collection.md): cast and reel, conditions, journals, rods and bait.
- [Party and minigames playbook](party-and-minigames.md): fast rounds, variety, fairness, empty-server fixes.
