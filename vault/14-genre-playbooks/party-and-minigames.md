# Party and minigames playbook

Party games run short rounds of varied minigames (races, elimination, quizzes, tag, hide-and-seek, floor-is-lava). They are friend-group magnets and great for clips, but they need a full server and a steady stream of fresh minigames.

Roblox genre: Party & Casual (subgenres Minigame, Childhood Game, Quiz, Coloring & Drawing).

## Audience

Young players and friend groups; social, casual. Sessions 10-25 minutes.

## The loop

```
Moment:  quick action under pressure -> laugh, win or lose
Round:   vote or random minigame -> 1-3 min round -> winners rewarded -> next round
Long:    currency for cosmetics, win counters, levels, new minigames each update
```

## Round design

- **Rounds of 1-3 minutes**, intermissions of 10-20 seconds.
- **Instantly understandable** minigames: one rule, shown with an icon and three words on screen.
- **Variety**: rotate between skill, luck, social and silly games so different players win.
- **Elimination games** should keep eliminated players busy (spectate, mini-activity, betting on winners with free currency).
- **Late joiners** enter at the next round with a countdown.
- **Voting** between 2-3 options gives agency without slowing things down.

## Fairness for mixed skill

- Mix luck into outcomes so newcomers win sometimes.
- Rewards for participation, not just wins.
- Team games that pair strong and new players.

## Meta-progression and monetization

Currency per round for cosmetics (hats, trails, win effects, emotes), levels with unlocks, private servers for friend groups (very important for party games), VIP, cosmetic passes. Avoid gameplay advantages.

## Retention

New minigames regularly (the core content), seasonal events and themed minigames, daily challenges, win leaderboards.

## Social

Friend parties stay together across rounds, team modes, emotes and celebrations, spectating.

## Empty servers

Party games die in empty servers. Use smaller max server sizes, fill servers before opening new ones, bot players in early stages, and minigames that work with 2 players.

## Clip moments

Funny eliminations, last-second wins, chaotic physics.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Long waits between rounds | Short intermissions, lobby activities |
| Minigames hard to understand | One rule, icon, three words |
| Same few minigames forever | Data-driven minigame framework to add new ones fast |
| Empty servers | Server size tuning, bots, 2-player-viable games |
