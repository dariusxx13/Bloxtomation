# Clip moments and shareability

Most Roblox breakouts grow partly off-platform: short videos on TikTok and YouTube Shorts, YouTubers playing the update, screenshots in Discord. A game that produces shareable moments gets free advertising. Since July 2025, new or returning-lapsed users you bring to Roblox through your share links, direct links or searches for your game's name can also pay you a 35% share of their first $100 of spending through Creator Rewards (with conditions). Shareability is now directly monetized.

## What makes a moment clip-worthy

A clip moment has at least three of these:

1. **Surprise**: something unexpected or very rare happens.
2. **Stakes**: something could be lost or won.
3. **Scale**: a huge number, a giant creature, a massive explosion, a ridiculous stack.
4. **Readability in one second**: a viewer scrolling past understands it without context or sound.
5. **Emotion on the avatar or in chat**: celebration, panic, betrayal, laughter.
6. **A twist**: the underdog wins, the thief gets caught, the "useless" item turns out legendary.

## Clip moment types that work on Roblox

| Type | Example | Systems that produce it |
|---|---|---|
| The impossible pull | A 1-in-10-million aura on the first roll | RNG with very rare tiers + big reveal |
| The heist | Stealing someone's best item with seconds to spare | Steal-and-defend, timers, chases |
| The comeback | Last player alive beats the monster | Round climax, last-stand mechanics |
| The flex | A giant pet army or a maxed base | Visible progression, show-off |
| The betrayal | Friend pushes friend off the tower | Physics, social tension |
| The scale shot | A number so large it breaks the counter | Exponential economies |
| The glitchy-funny | Ragdoll physics chaos | Physics-heavy mechanics |
| The scare | A jump scare in co-op horror | Horror pacing |
| The event | Whole server watches a sky event or boss | Live events, admin events |

Plan at least one of these into the core loop. "The clip moment" belongs in every pitch. See [Pitch and greenlight](../15-market-research/pitch-and-greenlight.md).

## Design rules

- **Make rare things look rare.** Unique colors, effects, sound, and a server announcement. A 1-in-a-million item that looks like a common one will never be clipped.
- **Let other players see it.** Broadcast rare drops in chat or with a banner; show the item on the character; give the moment a brief world effect others can see.
- **Make it readable without UI.** Viewers watch on phones with the game's HUD tiny. Put the drama in the world: size, color, light, motion.
- **Give names to things.** Named pets, auras and bosses are searchable and quotable ("I got the Celestial!").
- **Leaderboards and records.** "Server record!" and global leaderboards make moments competitive.
- **Recordable camera.** Avoid cameras that hide the action; provide a free-look or emote moment after big events. Studio's freecam (Left Shift + P in a solo playtest) is the tool for capturing your own trailers and thumbnails.

## Turning clips into players

- **Share links.** Create share links in Creator Hub for each channel (TikTok, YouTube, Discord) and each creator you work with. They are trackable in Acquisition analytics, can carry custom launch data (for example a welcome reward), and new or returning-lapsed users who join through them can count toward Creator Rewards audience expansion. See [User acquisition](../12-live-ops-and-launch/user-acquisition.md).
- **Codes.** Redeemable codes posted in video descriptions and Discord are a Roblox tradition. They give viewers a reason to join now. Keep codes small (a boost, not a game-breaker), time-limited, and validate them on the server.
- **Name-search.** Creator Rewards also counts new or returning-lapsed users who search your game's exact name and play. A short, distinctive, searchable name helps. See [Titles and descriptions](../11-store-page-and-discovery/titles-and-descriptions.md).
- **Creator partnerships.** Give YouTubers and TikTokers early access, exclusive cosmetics for their communities, or a custom code. Roblox runs a Video Stars program for influencers.
- **In-game sharing.** Invite prompts with launch data, the friend referral system with rewards for both sides, and in-game photo or emote moments. See [Social systems](../10-retention-and-social/social-systems.md).

> [!WARNING]
> Do not reward players for posting on social media in exchange for in-game items gated behind the post, and never use bots, alt accounts or teleport tricks to inflate visits or referrals. Creator Rewards explicitly forbids artificial activity and encouraging alts; violations can forfeit rewards or terminate accounts.

## Measuring shareability

- Share link clicks, signups, reactivations and spenders (Creator Hub > Analytics > Share Links).
- Acquisition source breakdown (how many plays come from outside Roblox).
- Search volume for your game's name (rising search is a sign of off-platform buzz).
- Code redemptions per code (which creator or post drove joins).

## For agents

- [ ] The pitch and GDD name the game's clip moment explicitly.
- [ ] The rarest outcome has unique visuals, sound and a server-wide broadcast.
- [ ] The clip moment is readable on a phone screen without UI.
- [ ] Share links exist per channel and per partner, with launch data where useful.
- [ ] Codes are validated server-side, one use per player, with expiry.
- [ ] No reward requires posting to social media; no artificial traffic of any kind.
