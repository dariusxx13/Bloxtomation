# Thumbnails

Thumbnails are the 16:9 images (and videos) shown on the Home page and at the top of your game's detail page. On the Home page they decide whether a player clicks. Play-through rate from Recommended for You is one of the most important ranking signals, and Roblox will not give Home recommendation impressions to a game with only a default thumbnail.

## Specs (platform facts, 2026)

- **16:9**, ideally **1920×1080**. Other ratios get stretched.
- Formats: jpg, png, gif, tga, bmp. Keep uploads for personalization under 3 MB.
- Up to **10** images or videos per game on the detail page.
- Avoid essential content at the **bottom** of the image; metadata like player counts can cover it.
- Alt text can be added for accessibility.
- All thumbnails are moderated.

## What makes a thumbnail work

A thumbnail is a poster, not a screenshot.

1. **One focal point.** A big character, creature or object, with a clear action or emotion.
2. **The fantasy, honestly shown.** The most exciting real moment of the game: the giant boss, the rare aura, the chase, the heist, the huge pet army. It must exist in the game.
3. **Motion and tension.** Diagonal composition, something about to happen, a character reacting.
4. **Contrast and color.** Saturated, bright for casual; strong lighting contrast for action and horror.
5. **Few words, big letters, or none.** A short word or two can help ("UPDATE!", the event name) but text is secondary to the image.
6. **Readable small.** On phones, Home tiles are small. Test at phone size.
7. **Distinct.** Your thumbnail should not look like every other game in your genre.

## Rules (from Roblox guidance)

- Must accurately reflect the game. Misleading thumbnails are demoted and can be moderated.
- Avoid ambiguous imagery and unclear graphics.
- Do not imply Robux or real-money rewards.
- Videos: authentic gameplay only (light camera work and post-processing allowed), no voiceover or songs with lyrics, no real-world footage, no claims like "50% off" or "Free UGC", limited overlay text describing gameplay. Video uploads have a monthly quota (rejections count against it). Videos are not shown on consoles or VR.

## Thumbnail personalization (Home page)

Platform facts:

- Activate **2 to 5** thumbnails on the Home Page tab; Roblox shows each to random users, learns which performs best for which user groups, and gives more impressions to the winners while still exploring.
- In Roblox's testing, games using personalization saw an average **+8.5%** qualified play-through rate (some much more).
- **Keep several thumbnails active** instead of picking one winner; tastes change over time.
- Roblox recommends testing a new set of thumbnails with every major update, then leaving them until the next update.
- The **Thumbnail Performance** table shows impressions, qualified plays, average playtime and qualified play-through rate per thumbnail.

How to use it:

1. Make 3-5 thumbnails that test genuinely different ideas (different hero, different moment, different color mood), not tiny variations.
2. Activate them together after an update.
3. After enough impressions, read which concepts win and for whom. Note that "qualified" play-through and average playtime matter: a clickbaity thumbnail with high clicks but short sessions is worse.
4. Apply the winning concept to the icon and future thumbnails.

## Production workflow

- Capture in Studio using **freecam** (Left Shift + P in a solo playtest) with good lighting, posed characters and effects.
- Composite in an image editor: add glow, depth of field, rim light, effects; keep it faithful to the game's look.
- Export at 1920×1080, check at phone size, check the bottom area is free of essentials.
- Store source files so thumbnails can be updated for events.

## For agents

- [ ] At least three distinct thumbnail concepts at 1920×1080 with a clear focal point.
- [ ] Each thumbnail shows real game content; no reward bait.
- [ ] Bottom area free of essential content; readable at phone size.
- [ ] Personalization active with 2-5 thumbnails; results read after each update.
- [ ] Winning concept fed back into the icon and ads creatives.
