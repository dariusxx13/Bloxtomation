# Store page video

Roblox lets you upload authentic gameplay videos as thumbnails. Once approved, a video appears first on your game's detail page and can also be shown on the Home page. The same footage, cut differently, feeds TikTok, Shorts and YouTube.

## Roblox's video rules (platform facts, 2026)

Allowed:

- Varied camera angles and cinematic camera work showing real gameplay (freecam helps).
- Minor post-processing (brightness, contrast, small effects) and your logo.
- Highlight-reel editing of real gameplay sequences.
- Your game's own UI text visible as part of gameplay.
- Music from Roblox's licensed audio catalog.

Not allowed (videos are rejected and count against the monthly upload quota of 3):

- Gameplay, UI or mechanics that are not in the game, or a misrepresented theme.
- Graphics enhanced beyond what players will see.
- Real-life footage of people or places, or other external content.
- Voiceover, narration, voice chat, or music with lyrics.
- Advertising text or claims ("50% off", "Free UGC!"); overlay text only sparingly to describe gameplay.

Videos are not shown on Xbox, PlayStation or VR (static thumbnails are).

## A structure that works (15-30 seconds)

| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0-2 s | The strongest image: the hero moment (rare drop, giant boss, heist) |
| 2-8 s | The core action, clearly, a few times, with juicy feedback |
| 8-15 s | Progression: before and after, getting stronger, new zone reveal |
| 15-22 s | Social and spectacle: friends together, server event, big numbers |
| 22-30 s | End on the best moment again; logo |

Rules of thumb:

- Assume no sound. The visuals must carry it.
- Fast cuts, but each shot readable (1-3 seconds).
- Show the phone-scale reality: avoid tiny details that only read on a monitor.
- Refresh the video with major updates.

## Social clips

Short vertical clips (9:16) for TikTok and Shorts follow a different rule: the first second must stop the scroll. Use the same clip-moment thinking as [Clip moments and shareability](../02-core-loop/clip-moments-and-shareability.md): one surprising, readable moment, then the payoff. Put a share link (and optionally a code) in the description, not in the video.

## Capturing footage

- Solo playtest in Studio with freecam (Left Shift + P) for cinematic angles.
- Use a test place with staged content (pets equipped, rare effects active, several test accounts).
- Record at 1080p or higher, 60 fps if possible.
- Keep raw footage organized by update so future trailers are quick.

## For agents

- [ ] Video shows only real gameplay at real visual quality; no voiceover, lyrics or ad claims.
- [ ] First two seconds show the strongest moment; the core action is clear by second eight.
- [ ] Readable without sound and at phone size.
- [ ] Video and social clips refreshed with each major update.
