# Endgame and long-term goals

Every game eventually runs out of "first times". The endgame is what veterans do then. Roblox's retention guidance ties D30 to exactly this: regular updates and social mechanics. Games that keep players for months combine endless systems (that never finish) with scheduled novelty (that keeps changing).

## Endless systems

| System | Why it never ends | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prestige layers | Each reset unlocks the next layer | See [Rebirth and prestige](../04-progression-and-economy/rebirth-and-prestige.md) |
| Collection completion with variants | Rare variants keep the chase going | See [Collection and variants](../03-rarity-and-rng/collection-and-variants.md) |
| Leaderboards and seasons | Rankings reset; there is always a new race | Seasonal resets give everyone a fresh start |
| Mastery and skill | Players improve their own play | Ranked modes, time trials, hard modes |
| Creative expression | Bases, outfits, houses keep changing | Roleplay, building, tycoon decoration |
| Trading markets | Values shift with every update | See [Trading economies](../04-progression-and-economy/trading-economies.md) |
| Social roles | Clan leader, top trader, helper | Status that depends on others |

## Scheduled novelty

- **Weekly updates** with something visible for veterans (a new egg, a new boss, a new variant), and monthly bigger updates (new zone, new system). See [Update cadence](../12-live-ops-and-launch/update-cadence.md).
- **Events** that bring time-limited goals and exclusive rewards. See [Events and seasons](../12-live-ops-and-launch/events-and-seasons.md).
- **Season passes** that give 4-8 week goals.
- **Hard modes** or "nightmare" versions of existing content for maxed players.

## Designing for veterans without breaking newcomers

- **Add at the top, keep the bottom fresh.** New top-tier content for veterans; refreshed early content and catch-up mechanics for newcomers.
- **Power creep control.** Each update raising power by a large factor makes old content trivial and the economy unstable. Prefer new *dimensions* (new collections, new systems, new modes) over bigger numbers.
- **Separate competitive spaces** (leaderboards by time window, matchmaking by level) so veterans and newcomers each have something to win.
- **Veterans as hosts.** Give veterans reasons to help new players (mentor rewards, raid carry bonuses). It improves newcomer retention and gives veterans status.

## Long-term goal ladder

Make sure every engaged player always has goals at three distances:

| Distance | Example |
|---|---|
| This session | Finish the daily quests, hatch 10 eggs, beat the weekly boss |
| This week | Complete the event track, reach the next rebirth tier, climb the weekly leaderboard |
| This month or season | Finish the season pass, complete a collection set, unlock the next world |

If a veteran cannot name a goal at each distance, they are close to leaving.

## Signals that the endgame is failing

- D30 falling while D1 and D7 hold.
- Average wallet balance of veterans rising (nothing to spend on).
- Leaderboards frozen (the same names, no movement).
- Community chatter like "nothing to do" or "waiting for the update".
- Sessions of veterans getting shorter.

## For agents

- [ ] At least two endless systems exist before launch, and a third is planned.
- [ ] Every update adds something visible to veterans and something for newcomers.
- [ ] Goal ladder (session, week, season) documented for a maxed player.
- [ ] Power growth per update estimated; new dimensions preferred over bigger multipliers.
- [ ] D30 and veteran wallet balances monitored as endgame health metrics.
