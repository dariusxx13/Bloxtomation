# Game concepts bank

These are **seeds**, not greenlit projects. Each combines a proven format with one fresh element, following [Anatomy of a Roblox hit](../21-hits-and-trends/anatomy-of-a-hit.md). All names and themes are original placeholders. Before any seed becomes a pitch it needs fresh chart evidence (PITCH-01 requires data no older than seven days), a twist check and a human decision ([Pitch and greenlight](pitch-and-greenlight.md)).

## How to use a seed

1. Score it with the hit-potential sheet in [Anatomy of a Roblox hit](../21-hits-and-trends/anatomy-of-a-hit.md).
2. Check the wave phase of its format with the chart tracker ([Trend waves](../21-hits-and-trends/trend-waves.md)).
3. Estimate the content floor and weekly update unit for the team ([Building an original anime game](../20-anime-games/building-an-original-anime-game.md) has the method).
4. Write the pitch with the [Pitch template](../18-templates-and-checklists/pitch-template.md).

## The seeds

### 1. Lighthouse Keepers

- **Loop:** keep your lighthouse lit through stormy nights by gathering fuel on a shared island, so ships reach port and pay you.
- **Format + twist:** co-op survival with a finish line (familiar) + every player owns a light that others' ships depend on (fresh).
- **Consequence:** neglect your light and a neighbour's ship wrecks, scattering loot anyone can grab.
- **Session:** 20-40 minutes per night cycle; long arc of upgrades.
- **Content floor:** one island, three storm types, ten upgrades.
- **Money:** lighthouse skins, beam colours, crew pets.
- **Risk:** survival co-op is crowded; needs a strong social hook.
- **Evidence:** co-op survival with a finish line (99 Nights in the Forest) ([Hits of 2025](../21-hits-and-trends/hits-2025.md)).

### 2. Night Market Stalls

- **Loop:** run a food stall in a night market, serve customers fast, and spot the "odd" customers who are not what they seem.
- **Format + twist:** job simulator (familiar) + anomaly detection with a shared market (fresh in market form).
- **Consequence:** serve an anomaly and the whole street's lights flicker; neighbouring stalls lose customers.
- **Session:** 10-minute shifts; ranks and stall upgrades.
- **Content floor:** six recipes, eight anomaly types, one street.
- **Money:** stall themes, uniforms, tip multipliers.
- **Risk:** novelty fade, as seen in 2026 job-anomaly games; needs a content runway.
- **Evidence:** Animal Hospital and the anomaly job format ([Job and shift simulators playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/job-and-shift-simulators.md)).

### 3. Kite Clash

- **Loop:** fly a kite higher every second, cut rival kites' strings, and collect rare kites from the sky.
- **Format + twist:** progress-every-second climbing (familiar) + light PvP string-cutting (fresh).
- **Consequence:** a cut string drops your kite to the ground for others to collect.
- **Session:** 5-10 minutes, high frequency.
- **Content floor:** one sky with altitude bands, 20 kites.
- **Money:** kite skins, trails, string effects.
- **Risk:** physics and netcode for strings; needs simple, readable collisions.
- **Evidence:** short-session progress formats ([Speed and escape courses playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/speed-and-escape-courses.md)).

### 4. Fossil Rush

- **Loop:** dig fossils on a shared quarry, assemble skeletons in your museum, and protect your rarest bones from night raiders.
- **Format + twist:** dig-and-collect (familiar) + museum displays others can raid at night (fresh).
- **Consequence:** unprotected bones can be taken; guards and traps defend.
- **Session:** 20-30 minutes; long collection arc.
- **Content floor:** one quarry, 30 bone sets, museum rooms.
- **Money:** museum themes, dig tools, display cases.
- **Risk:** theft games need strong anti-exploit and newcomer protection.
- **Evidence:** steal-and-defend layers on collection loops ([Steal and defend playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/steal-and-defend.md)).

### 5. Weather Workshop

- **Loop:** roll weather cores, place them on your island to grow strange crops, and trade the rare mutations.
- **Format + twist:** RNG roll (familiar) + rolled items change your farm's weather and crops (fresh).
- **Consequence:** storms you cause spill into neighbouring plots, helping or hurting them.
- **Session:** idle with check-ins; weekly weather events.
- **Content floor:** eight weather types, 25 crops, mutation table.
- **Money:** luck boosts (with disclosed odds), plot expansions, cosmetic skies.
- **Risk:** paid random item compliance; economy inflation.
- **Evidence:** RNG and grow formats ([RNG and aura playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/rng-and-aura.md), [Grow and farm playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/grow-and-farm.md)).

### 6. Relay Racers

- **Loop:** form a team of four, pass a baton through absurd obstacle legs, and race other teams.
- **Format + twist:** obby (familiar) + team relay where each player runs one leg (fresh).
- **Social:** everyone matters for exactly one leg; friends cheer from the stands.
- **Session:** 4-minute races.
- **Content floor:** 12 legs that combine into tracks.
- **Money:** batons, outfits, celebrations.
- **Risk:** needs quick team formation with bots or uneven teams.
- **Evidence:** party and sports rounds ([Party and minigames playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/party-and-minigames.md), [Sports games playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/sports-games.md)).

### 7. Shardbearers (original anime-style)

- **Loop:** roll Shards to unlock bearers, clear sky islands with them, and awaken your strongest bearer.
- **Format + twist:** anime dice and roll loop (familiar) + Strain as a shared stamina meter that makes fights a resource puzzle (fresh).
- **Session:** short rolls, medium stages.
- **Content floor:** 40 bearers, five islands.
- **Money:** fair gacha with pity and shard shop; skins.
- **Risk:** crowded anime space; must stay original.
- **Evidence:** the worked example world in [Anime power systems and worldbuilding](../20-anime-games/anime-power-systems-and-worldbuilding.md).

### 8. Tiny Titans Arena

- **Loop:** pick a toy-sized hero with a simple moveset, battle on household-object arenas, and unlock new toys.
- **Format + twist:** battlegrounds (familiar) + giant everyday arenas (a kitchen, a bathtub) as hazards (fresh).
- **Session:** drop-in arena fights.
- **Content floor:** six heroes, three arenas.
- **Money:** skins, finishers, emotes.
- **Risk:** combat feel must be excellent from day one.
- **Evidence:** battlegrounds evergreens ([Anime battlegrounds and fighting deep dive](../20-anime-games/anime-battlegrounds-and-fighting.md)).

### 9. Cloud Couriers

- **Loop:** deliver parcels across floating towns on a glider, faster deliveries unlock better gliders and routes.
- **Format + twist:** driving and delivery (familiar) + gliding and wind currents (fresh).
- **Social:** co-op big deliveries; races on routes.
- **Session:** 10-20 minutes.
- **Content floor:** one region with three towns.
- **Money:** gliders, trails, cosmetic parcels.
- **Risk:** movement must feel good on touch.
- **Evidence:** vehicle and ride formats ([Driving and vehicles playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/driving-and-vehicles.md)).

### 10. Haunted House Flippers

- **Loop:** buy cursed houses, clear the haunting with friends, renovate and sell for profit.
- **Format + twist:** tycoon and renovation (familiar) + co-op ghost clearing (fresh).
- **Consequence:** sloppy clearing leaves a ghost that follows you to your next house.
- **Session:** 15-30 minutes per house.
- **Content floor:** five houses, ten ghost types, a renovation catalogue.
- **Money:** furniture packs, tools, cosmetic ghosts.
- **Risk:** horror intensity versus audience age.
- **Evidence:** horror's strength in October 2026 ([October 2026 chart snapshot](../21-hits-and-trends/chart-snapshot-2026-10.md)).

### 11. Pocket Puzzle League (2D)

- **Loop:** solve quick 2D tile puzzles in head-to-head matches; win to climb leagues and unlock puzzle themes.
- **Format + twist:** puzzle (familiar) + Roblox-native social matches and turn notifications (fresh, using the 2D and asynchronous features announced at RDC 2026).
- **Session:** 2-5 minutes, or asynchronous turns.
- **Content floor:** two puzzle types, 200 generated levels.
- **Money:** themes, boards, emotes.
- **Risk:** depends on announced features; check their release status first ([Engine and platform features 2024-2026](../22-references/engine-features-2024-2026.md)).
- **Evidence:** Roblox's stated push into 2D and puzzle games ([Platform shifts 2025-2026](../21-hits-and-trends/platform-shifts-2025-2026.md)).

### 12. Beekeeper Bandits

- **Loop:** raise bee colonies that make honey while you are away, raid rival hives for royal jelly, defend yours.
- **Format + twist:** idle production (familiar) + raids on small servers of six (fresh combination).
- **Consequence:** raids steal honey, never bees, so losses sting but do not erase progress.
- **Session:** idle with active raid windows.
- **Content floor:** 15 bee types, hive upgrades, three flower zones.
- **Money:** hive skins, colony slots, offline boosts.
- **Risk:** balancing raids so newcomers are protected.
- **Evidence:** small servers and consequence layers in 2026 hits ([Viral and social mechanics](../21-hits-and-trends/viral-and-social-mechanics.md)).

## Adding a seed

New seeds follow the same fields: loop, format plus twist, consequence or social layer, session, content floor, money, risk, evidence. Seeds that copy a specific game's name, characters or presentation are rejected.

## For agents

- [ ] Seeds are scored and wave-checked before any pitch work.
- [ ] Names and themes stay original; evidence lines cite vault notes with dated sources.
