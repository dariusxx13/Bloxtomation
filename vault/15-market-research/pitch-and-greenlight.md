# Pitch and greenlight

Most of a game's fate is decided before the first line of code: the idea, the audience, the timing and the twist. A good pitch process prevents months spent on games that could never work and gives every idea a fair, comparable evaluation.

## What a pitch must contain

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Name and one-liner | "You ___ to get ___ so you can ___." |
| Genre and subgenre | Roblox genre the game would select, and why (chart visibility) |
| Target audience | Age tier (Kids, Select, 16+), devices, tourist vs local appeal |
| Core loop | Moment, session and long loops |
| First 60 seconds | What the player does and sees |
| Goal at 10-15 minutes | The first named goal, as a player action |
| Minutes 10-30 | What keeps them going |
| Why tomorrow | The return hook |
| Friend reason | Why it is better with friends |
| Clip moment | The moment players will record |
| How it makes money | Main products and why they are fair |
| Icon concept | What the 150-pixel icon shows |
| Evidence | Chart snapshots, comparable games with numbers, dates and sources (no older than 7 days) |
| Competition | Closest comparable games, their size and like ratio, and how we differ |
| Urgency | Early or late in the trend, with evidence (see [Trend lifecycle](trend-lifecycle.md)) |
| Twist | What changes how it plays compared with comparables |
| Build estimate | Scope of a first playable and of launch content |
| Risks | Top 3 risks (design, tech, policy, IP, market) and mitigations |
| Reuse | Existing systems and assets from our library we can reuse |

## Scoring rubric

Score each criterion 0-3 and multiply by its weight. Use the same rubric for every idea so they can be compared.

| Criterion | Weight | 0 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core loop clarity and fun (tested or comparable evidence) | 3 | Unclear, untested | Prototype or strong comparable proves it |
| First-session strength | 2 | No clear first minute | Fun in 10 s, goal in 15 min |
| Return and endgame potential | 2 | One-session game | Endless systems, clear update path |
| Social and clip potential | 2 | Solo, no moments | Co-play reason and strong clip moment |
| Market evidence | 2 | None or stale | Fresh, multi-game evidence of demand |
| Differentiation | 2 | Clone | Clear twist that changes play |
| Timing | 1 | Late, saturated | Early, rising |
| Monetization fit | 1 | Weak or unfair | Natural, fair, multiple tiers |
| Feasibility for our team | 2 | Beyond our skills or budget | Comfortably within, reuses our library |
| Policy and IP safety | 1 | Licensing or rating issues | Clean |

Maximum: 54. Rules of thumb: 40+ build a prototype now; 30-39 refine the pitch (usually the twist or evidence); under 30 park it.

Scores are aids to judgment, not decisions. A human makes the greenlight call.

## The prototype gate

Greenlight means **build a prototype**, not the whole game. The prototype answers one question: is the core loop fun?

- Scope: the moment loop with juice, one upgrade line, one zone, placeholder art. Days, not weeks.
- Test: 5+ fresh players (or kid-tester style agent playtests), recorded first sessions, the seven loop tests from [Core loop design](../02-core-loop/core-loop-design.md).
- Decision: continue to a full build, pivot the loop, or stop. Stopping a weak prototype is a success of the process.

## Alternatives and portfolio

- Pitch two or three alternatives at a time and compare them side by side.
- Until the first game is live, approve one pitch at a time; keep tracking the others (trends move, and a parked pitch may become urgent).
- Keep a portfolio record: what we shipped, what worked, house patterns. New pitches state whether they follow house patterns or argue for a deviation.

## For agents

- [ ] Pitch includes every field in the table; evidence cites sources and dates no older than 7 days.
- [ ] Rubric scored with one-line justifications per criterion.
- [ ] Twist and urgency read are explicit.
- [ ] Pitch checked against our portfolio (not something we already ship).
- [ ] Nothing gets built beyond a prototype without human approval.
