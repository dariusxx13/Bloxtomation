# Hits of 2026

The 2026 charts mixed three stories: brand-new breakouts (Animal Hospital, Steal An Egg, the +1 Speed games), sequels and launches with big starts and fast fades (Grow a Garden 2, Anime Expeditions), and veterans setting new records through events (Murder Mystery 2). Numbers are third-party tracker readings reported by Bloxodes and StudioKrew (citing GGAID, Rolimon's, RoVitals and rblxdb) and the studio's own chart snapshot; they are approximate and dated.

## Animal Hospital: the horror job that took over the platform

**The game.** Night shifts at a veterinary clinic: register patients, treat them correctly, handle emergencies. Some patients are not what they seem. A sanity meter (topped up with coffee) replaces health; ten classes such as Intern, Surgeon, Security and Head Nurse change treatment speed, items and camera access (StudioKrew, July 2026).

**Timeline.**

| Date | Milestone | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-05-10 | Released | StudioKrew, July 2026 |
| 2026-06-09 | About 884 average players | Bloxodes, June 2026 |
| 2026-06-30 | About 429,721 average players (daily high about 563,664) | Bloxodes, June 2026 |
| 2026-07-05 | A reported peak of about 737K | StudioKrew citing Rolimon's |
| August 2026 | About 358K players; reported as the most recommended game by Roblox's official placements | StudioKrew citing GGAID |
| September 2026 | People's Choice, Best New Game, Builderman Award and Innovation in Creative Direction at the Innovation Awards | Press lists |
| September 2026 | Down about 90% month over month to about 35K despite an expansion and a double-coins weekend | StudioKrew, September 2026 |

**Why it broke out.** A fresh premise (a job sim with a horror twist), co-op asymmetry (friends need different classes), clip-friendly scares, and a fan-art and meme wave on TikTok (Bloxodes, June 2026; StudioKrew, July 2026). Every week-over-week comparison in June was positive (Bloxodes).

**Why it faded.** No single trigger is visible in public data; the tracker's read was novelty fade (StudioKrew, September 2026). The lesson stands either way: **awards and recommendations do not guarantee retention** once the premise has been fully experienced.

**Lessons.**

1. A new job or role fantasy with a twist can still break out from zero in weeks.
2. Asymmetric co-op (different roles) gives friends a reason to play together.
3. Horror-adjacent games need a long-term structure (unlocks, new shifts, lore arcs) or they burn out after players have seen the scares ([Survival and horror co-op playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/survival-horror-coop.md)).

## The +1 Speed games: progress you can feel every step

**The format.** Every step adds +1 speed; you race through an escape course or climb walls, rebirth to push further. Progress is a number that only goes up.

| Game | Facts | Source |
|---|---|---|
| +1 Speed Keyboard Escape | Released January 2026; a 97% rating from about 9.1M votes; about 4.4B visits by August; an all-time high of about 7.28M concurrent players and a 24-hour peak of about 5.2M on 2026-08-09 from admin events; average session about 8 minutes; about 771,768 average players on 2026-06-27 | StudioKrew (July-August 2026); Bloxodes (June 2026) |
| +1 Speed Monkey Escape | Released 2026-05-10; a 98% rating from about 1.48M votes; an all-time peak of about 1.09M | StudioKrew, August 2026 |
| +1 Stone Skipping | About 63K players, created in September 2026 | Studio chart snapshot, 2026-10-24 |

**Notable practices.** Rotating multiplier tags in the title (for example "X10" then "X2") that advertise live boosts to anyone scrolling the charts (StudioKrew, September 2026). One game reportedly gated free speed boosts behind joining an off-platform community and liking the game; note that Roblox does not allow off-platform links inside games, and rewards for engagement actions are a policy question for the human ([Anime community, updates and codes](../20-anime-games/anime-community-updates-and-codes.md)).

**Lessons.**

1. **Visible progress every step** is one of the strongest hooks on the platform.
2. Short sessions (about 8 minutes) can still make a top-five game when frequency is high.
3. Near-identical games from unrelated studios charting together is the clearest sign of a format wave ([Trend waves](trend-waves.md)).

## Grow a Garden 2: a sequel's huge start and fast fall

**Facts.** Launched 2026-06-12 and passed 400K concurrent players within 20 minutes; about 505K players and 1.5B visits six weeks later; average session about 59 minutes (StudioKrew citing Rolimon's, July 2026). Its strongest week in August averaged about 482,533 players and its weakest about 27,965, with every same-weekday comparison down (Bloxodes, August 2026). Out of the GGAID top 50 by the end of September (StudioKrew).

**What changed from the original.** Night raids on other gardens with defensive plants and pets; a circular map where every farm faces a shared hub (StudioKrew, July 2026); a second world in a month-long fall event (StudioKrew, August 2026).

**Lessons.**

1. A famous name buys a launch, not retention.
2. A large event can slow a decline without reversing it.
3. Adding a consequence layer to a known loop is a strong idea, but it does not replace the novelty that made the original explode.

## Steal An Egg: the 2026 record holder

**The game.** Steal eggs, hatch them into pets, earn money, train speed on a treadmill to raid richer areas and outrun other players. Servers hold seven players (StudioKrew, August 2026).

**Timeline.**

| Date | Milestone | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-25 | Released | StudioKrew |
| 2026-08-20 | About 282K players, about 109M visits | StudioKrew citing GGAID |
| September 2026 | A new update every Saturday with an admin session | StudioKrew |
| 2026-09-19 | A crossover with the creator of Steal a Brainrot; a reported peak of about 14.27M | StudioKrew citing RoVitals |
| 2026-09-30 | About 1.6M players, roughly five times the second-placed game | StudioKrew citing GGAID and rblxdb |
| 2026-10-24 | About 1.6M players and about 9.3B visits; second in the snapshot and third in the top-earning sort | Studio chart snapshot |

**Why it worked.** A compact theft-and-hatch loop combining two proven formats, tiny servers that make theft personal, a weekly Saturday heartbeat, and a crossover with the creator of 2025's biggest theft game.

**What it spawned.** By October 2026 the charts showed a wave of egg-themed games, such as "Break and Steal an Egg", "Fake an Egg", "Climb For Animals Eggs" and "Bike For Egg" (studio snapshot; RoWatcher weekly report, 2026-10-12 to 10-18). See [Trend waves](trend-waves.md).

## Ride A Pet: a smart twist on the egg formula

**Facts.** Find eggs around the map, hatch them, then ride what you hatched; faster mounts reach rarer eggs, so speed is both the progression stat and transport. Updates on 2026-09-19 (weather mutations) and 2026-09-26 (a volcano island with an egg that needs a flying pet); about 208K players and a 97% rating at the end of September; servers of six (StudioKrew, September 2026).

**Lesson.** The best twists make one system do two jobs (here, speed is both power and travel).

## Murder Mystery 2: a 2014 game sets its record in 2026

**Facts.** Its daily average sat around 252K-301K for most of July 2026, then jumped when its Summer 2026 event opened on July 23 at 21:00 UTC, staying above 511K daily for the rest of the month (Bloxodes, July 2026). In August it set a new all-time peak of about 1.35M (StudioKrew citing GGAID), with a strongest rolling week averaging about 1.01M (Bloxodes, August 2026). On 2026-10-24 it led the studio's snapshot with about 1.66M players during its Halloween period.

**Why.** A seasonal event with mystery boxes and a premium item pack revalued a trading economy of collectible knives and guns built over twelve years; players who no longer play rounds still log in to trade (StudioKrew, July and August 2026).

**Lessons.** A **trading economy with history** turns every seasonal drop into a platform-wide event. Old games are not finished games ([Evergreen giants](evergreen-giants.md)).

## What 2026 taught so far

| Lesson | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Brand-new premises still break out from zero | Animal Hospital, Steal An Egg, +1 Speed games |
| Novelty fades fast without long-term structure | Animal Hospital, Grow a Garden 2, Anime Expeditions |
| Events revalue old economies | Murder Mystery 2's summer record |
| Formats become waves within weeks | +1 Speed and egg games |
| Crossovers between hits create records | Steal An Egg with the creator of Steal a Brainrot |
| Cadence is marketing | Anime Dice, Steal An Egg, Brookhaven |
