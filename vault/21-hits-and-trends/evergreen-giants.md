# Evergreen giants

Breakouts get headlines; evergreens pay the bills for years. In 2025 Brookhaven and Blox Fruits were in the monthly top 10 every single month, and RIVALS and The Strongest Battlegrounds in 11 of 12 months (Newzoo, data from RoMonitor). In July and August 2026 the most-played game on the platform was Murder Mystery 2, launched in 2014. This note asks what these games share and what a new game can borrow.

## Six reasons games last

### 1. They are social institutions

Brookhaven has no objective; players invent their own stories in a shared town. A place where friends meet does not run out of content because the content is the people. Its developer ships an update almost every Friday (vehicles, houses, themed zones), and it set an all-time record of about 1.3M concurrent players in December 2025 (StudioKrew, July 2026). In September 2026 it ran a two-week event with a major dinosaur film brand (StudioKrew, September 2026). See [Roleplay and life sim playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/roleplay-life-sim.md).

### 2. They have trading economies with history

Murder Mystery 2's knives and guns, Adopt Me's pets, Blox Fruits' fruits and Pet Simulator 99's pets all carry community-assigned values built over years. Every new seasonal item revalues the whole market, so even players who stopped playing log in to trade (StudioKrew, July and August 2026). Adopt Me turns daily pet care into long-term goals: four grown pets combine into a Neon, four Neons into a Mega Neon, and every pet is a trade asset (StudioKrew, September 2026). See [Trading economies](../04-progression-and-economy/trading-economies.md).

**Key insight:** a new game's event has nothing to revalue; a twelve-year-old game's event revalues everything. Murder Mystery 2's summer 2026 event produced its all-time record while a much newer sequel with a month-long event kept falling (StudioKrew, August 2026).

### 3. They reward skill

RIVALS (short duels), The Strongest Battlegrounds (combat feel), DOORS (learning monsters), Tower of Hell (a hard obby). Mastery is renewable content: players come back to get better, to beat friends and to climb ranks ([Shooters and PvP playbook](../14-genre-playbooks/shooters-and-pvp.md)).

### 4. They keep a public calendar

| Game | Calendar (as reported by trackers in 2026) |
|---|---|
| Brookhaven | Updates almost every Friday, midday US Eastern |
| Adopt Me! | Updates every Friday at 11:05 AM ET, admin giveaways every Saturday, a weekly "2D Tuesday" fixture |
| 99 Nights in the Forest | Saturday update parties; content about every two weeks |
| RIVALS | Seasonal and birthday events; community-sourced cosmetics |
| Blox Fruits | Long cycles with very large updates; quieter months held up by trading |

Players plan around a predictable calendar. The studio behind Adopt Me also says it uses Roblox's analytics and experiments to test features (Roblox newsroom feature, cited by Bloxodes, August 2026). See [Update cadence](../12-live-ops-and-launch/update-cadence.md).

### 5. They run seasonal events that bring people home

Halloween, winter and summer events are the evergreens' biggest moments: Murder Mystery 2's Summer 2026 event; Adopt Me's Halloween event placed on Roblox's official calendar for October 3, 2026 (StudioKrew, September 2026); Flee the Facility's summer event in July 2026 (Bloxodes). In the October 2026 snapshot, many evergreens carried Halloween tags in their titles. See [Events and seasons](../12-live-ops-and-launch/events-and-seasons.md).

### 6. They keep collections growing

New pets, knives, fish, outfits and characters extend collections without resetting anything. Collectors rarely quit a collection that keeps growing ([Collection and variants](../03-rarity-and-rng/collection-and-variants.md)).

| Game | Comeback | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Shindo Life | July 2026: every usable weekly comparison positive, typical weekly gain about 101% | Bloxodes, July 2026 |
| Build A Boat For Treasure (2016) | June 2026: up in 17 of 18 weekly comparisons, nearly 90% from quietest to strongest week | Bloxodes, June 2026 |
| Tower of Hell (2018) | Up in every June comparison; steady gains in July and August 2026 | Bloxodes |
| Bee Swarm Simulator (2018) | A quiet, steady gain through July 2026 | Bloxodes |
| DOORS | Daily average about 84,558 on 2026-08-29, a day after a major update | Bloxodes, August 2026 |
| Lumber Tycoon 2 and Jailbreak | Waves during Roblox's 20th anniversary event, The Hunt (September 2026) | Bloxodes, September 2026 |

Comebacks follow content, events and platform moments. Lapsed audiences for old games are enormous; a returning-player reward and a visible "new" signal can wake them ([Retention systems](../10-retention-and-social/retention-systems.md)).

## Evergreens also wobble

- Adopt Me fell about 13% per week through July 2026, then rose about 14% per week in August with its update windows (Bloxodes).
- Blox Fruits fell on every comparable weekday after its September 2026 mega-update spike (Bloxodes, September 2026).
- Brookhaven's player counts show a strong seasonal shape (StudioKrew, August 2026).

A weekly dip is not death. Judge trends over months and against the same weekday ([Reading the charts](../15-market-research/reading-the-charts.md)).

## What a new game can borrow

| Evergreen trait | How to build it in from day one |
|---|---|
| Social institution | A shared space where friends meet: hubs, homes, show-off areas ([Lobby and hub design](../06-world-and-look/lobby-and-hub-design.md)) |
| Trading with history | Tradable, limited items released over time; secure trading; values the community can track |
| Skill | A mastery path that does not depend on spending |
| Calendar | A fixed weekly update and event time announced in game |
| Seasons | Halloween, winter and summer plans in the roadmap from launch |
| Growing collections | A content pipeline that adds collectibles weekly |

## For agents

- [ ] Every pitch names which evergreen traits the game will build and when.
- [ ] Roadmaps include the seasonal calendar for the first year.
- [ ] Trend judgements on old games compare the same weekday over several weeks.
