# Anatomy of a Roblox hit

[The hit formula](../00-start-here/hit-formula.md) lists twelve timeless principles. This note looks at the **evidence from the 2025-2026 breakouts** (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights in the Forest, Dead Rails, Animal Hospital, the +1 Speed games, Steal An Egg, Anime Dice and others) and pulls out what they had in common. Use it to judge pitches and to decide what to build next. The case studies themselves are in [Hits of 2025](hits-2025.md) and [Hits of 2026](hits-2026.md).

## How big "big" became

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Platform concurrent record | About 47.3M users on 2025-08-23 | Press coverage and Wikipedia articles on Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot |
| Highest single-game peak reported | About 25.4M concurrent players (Steal a Brainrot, October 2025) | Press coverage (PocketGamer.biz) |
| Roblox 2025 revenue and bookings | About $4.9B revenue (+36%), $6.8B bookings (+55%) | Newzoo summary of public results, April 2026 |
| Average daily active users in 2025 | About 126.5M (+53%) | Same |
| Hours spent in 2025 | About 123.8B (+69%) | Same |
| Concentration | The largest title's share of top-10 visits rose from 22% (Q1 2025) to 43% (Q4 2025); by year end three games held over 70% of top-10 visits | Newzoo, data from RoMonitor |
| Charting players in the studio's snapshot | About 15.1M concurrent players across 878 charting games on 2026-10-24, 17:04 UTC | Studio chart tracker |

Hits are bigger than ever, and the top is more concentrated. The prize for a real hit is enormous; the median game still gets very little.

## The ten recurring ingredients

### 1. The title explains the loop

"Grow a Garden", "Steal a Brainrot", "99 Nights in the Forest", "+1 Speed Keyboard Escape", "Steal An Egg", "Ride A Pet", "Merge a Nuke!", "Kick a Lucky Block", "Chop A Forest". A child scrolling a phone understands the game from three or four words. The verb is in the title.

**Test:** can the game's name be the one-sentence pitch?

### 2. Familiar, then fresh

Analyses of Dead Rails described it as a known format (building and defending a moving vehicle on a hostile journey, as in older hits) with a fresh setting (a Wild West zombie apocalypse on a train) (GameAnalytics blog, 2026). Grow a Garden is a farming idle game with offline growth and mutations; Steal a Brainrot is a tycoon with capture-the-flag theft and meme characters. Each is one twist away from something players already understand. Designers call this "most advanced yet acceptable".

**Test:** name the familiar format and the one fresh thing ([Trend lifecycle](../15-market-research/trend-lifecycle.md)).

### 3. A consequence layer

Many 2025-2026 hits add **risk of loss caused by other players** to a cosy loop: stealing (Steal a Brainrot, Steal An Egg), night raids on gardens (Grow a Garden 2), hidden threats among patients in a co-op job (Animal Hospital). Trackers observing the July 2026 charts called social risk "the mechanic of the year" (StudioKrew, July 2026). Loss creates stories, grudges, revenge and clips, and gives players a reason to come back and defend what they have.

**Test:** what can another player take from me, and how do I protect it?

### 4. Progress visible every second

+1 Speed games add speed with every step and show the number rising constantly. Idle games show income ticking. Hatching shows a reveal every few seconds. The player never waits long for the next number to go up ([Visible, constant progress](../00-start-here/hit-formula.md)).

### 5. Collectibles with rarity and surprise

Eggs and pets, brainrots, crops with mutations, units, dice rolls. Collection gives long-term goals, rarity gives excitement and show-off value, and mutations or variants make even common items occasionally special ([Collection and variants](../03-rarity-and-rng/collection-and-variants.md)).

### 6. Small, personal servers

Steal An Egg caps servers at seven players and Ride A Pet at six (StudioKrew, August and September 2026). Small servers make theft personal and make every player visible. Big lobbies hide players in a crowd. Server size is a design decision, not a default ([Social systems](../10-retention-and-social/social-systems.md)).

### 7. A weekly heartbeat

Nearly every 2026 breakout runs a fixed weekly rhythm: Steal An Egg and Anime Dice with Saturday updates and admin sessions; Steal a Brainrot with Saturday events whose averages ran well above its monthly average (Bloxodes, June 2026); 99 Nights in the Forest with Saturday update parties; Brookhaven with Friday updates. See [Live events and admin events](live-events-and-admin-events.md).

### 8. Outside distribution

- **Roblox's own social channels.** Analyses of Dead Rails point to Roblox's official TikTok, Instagram and X posts in late February 2025 as the moment it jumped to the front page (GameAnalytics blog, 2026).
- **Creators.** Large Roblox YouTubers posting videos within days multiplied the effect (same source).
- **Fan content.** Animal Hospital's growth was linked to fan animations and memes on TikTok and a wave tracked by Know Your Meme (StudioKrew, July 2026; Bloxodes, June 2026).
- **Crossovers between hits.** Steal An Egg's crossover with the creator of Steal a Brainrot coincided with its record peak (StudioKrew, September 2026).

Plan the external funnel from day one ([User acquisition](../12-live-ops-and-launch/user-acquisition.md)).

### 9. Retention the algorithm rewards

Roblox's recommendation system favours long-term retention and monetization of players who arrive organically through Home recommendations; engagement from ad-acquired players is excluded from those ranking signals (Fact, creator docs, accessed 2026-10-24). Trackers reported Animal Hospital as the game with the most official recommendation placements in August 2026 (StudioKrew citing GGAID). Hits keep players coming back on day 2-7 and beyond, not only on day 1 ([Discovery algorithm](../11-store-page-and-discovery/discovery-algorithm.md)).

### 10. Low barrier, high clarity

Several mega-hits used simple, even old-school visuals (Grow a Garden's studded textures echo early Roblox). What they never lacked was **clarity**: one obvious thing to do, readable on a phone, immediate feedback. Polish of feel and clarity beats graphical fidelity on this platform ([Game feel and juice](../02-core-loop/game-feel-and-juice.md)).

## Session length versus session frequency

Trackers reported very different session shapes among top games in July 2026: Grow a Garden 2 averaging roughly 59 minutes per session and +1 Speed Keyboard Escape about 8 minutes (StudioKrew citing Rolimon's). Both were in the top four. They are different businesses:

| Long sessions (idle, farming, RPG) | Short sessions (+1 speed, obby, party) |
|---|---|
| Offline progress, long goals, premium currency sinks | Impulse purchases, instant boosts, cosmetics |
| Retention through investment | Retention through habit and frequency |
| Monetization through progression | Monetization inside a single sitting |

Decide which one you are building before designing the economy ([AFK, idle and short-session games](afk-idle-and-short-session-games.md)).

## Who made the hits

Hits do not require large studios. Grow a Garden's first version was reportedly built in about three days by a 16-year-old developer, and a studio bought a share when it had around 1,000 concurrent players (Wikipedia, citing press interviews). What followed (weekly events, dedicated event teams, partnerships) required scale. The pattern: **small team finds the loop, bigger team scales the live operation** ([Studio business models](studio-business-models.md)).

## Hit potential scoring sheet

Score a pitch or a live game 0-2 on each line (0 = absent, 1 = partial, 2 = strong). Use with the twelve principles in [The hit formula](../00-start-here/hit-formula.md).

| # | Ingredient | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title explains the loop | |
| 2 | Familiar format plus one fresh thing | |
| 3 | Consequence layer from other players (or a clear reason it is not needed) | |
| 4 | Progress visible every few seconds | |
| 5 | Collectibles with rarity and surprise | |
| 6 | Server size chosen for the social design | |
| 7 | Weekly heartbeat planned (cadence, events) | |
| 8 | External funnel planned (creators, social, crossovers) | |
| 9 | D2-D7 retention hooks designed | |
| 10 | Clear on a phone in ten seconds | |

**Reading:** 16-20 strong hit potential; 11-15 promising with gaps to close; 10 or less, rethink before building. A high score is not a prediction; it means the pitch has the shape of recent hits.
