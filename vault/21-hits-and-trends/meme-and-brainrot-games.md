# Meme and brainrot games

Young players live inside internet culture, and Roblox games that catch a meme at the right moment can explode. The 2025 "Italian brainrot" meme (absurd AI-generated creatures with mock-Italian names and chanted voice lines) became the theme of Steal a Brainrot, which went on to the highest concurrent player count of any Roblox game ([Hits of 2025](hits-2025.md)). This note covers how to ride meme culture without copying anyone's work.

## Why meme characters spread

- **Instant recognition.** Kids already know the characters from short videos; the game feels familiar before the first click.
- **Sound.** Memes often come with a chant or catchphrase; sound makes clips recognisable with the screen off.
- **Absurdity.** Ridiculous names and designs are funny to say and share.
- **Collectability.** A roster of silly characters with rarities is a natural collection.
- **Clip fuel.** Children's reactions to losing a favourite character became viral content in their own right (Wikipedia, citing press).

## Memes have owners

A meme is not public domain because it is everywhere. Characters, images, animations and sounds have creators, and some have agencies representing them.

- In early September 2025, Steal a Brainrot's developer removed one meme character after a **licensing dispute with its creator and a brand agency**; the character returned on 2025-11-29 (Wikipedia, citing press).
- The same game is registered with the U.S. Copyright Office, and its studio sued an unlicensed copy on another platform in October 2025 (Wikipedia). The theft loop, title and presentation became protected assets of their own.

**For this studio:** using a third-party meme character is an IP decision for the human. Agents do not import, recreate or prompt AI tools for named meme characters; they design original ones ([IP and licensing](../17-policy-and-business/ip-and-licensing.md)).

## Designing original meme-able characters

The meme format can be used without anyone's characters.

| Ingredient | How to make it original |
|---|---|
| Absurd mash-up | Combine two unrelated things (an animal and an object, a food and a vehicle) in your own design |
| A name that is fun to say | Rhythmic, repetitive or mock-language names you invent; search them first to avoid collisions |
| A signature sound | A short original chant or noise recorded or composed for the game, owned by the group |
| A signature move | A dance, wobble or pose that players recognise in clips |
| Rarity ladder | From mildly silly to absurdly grand; the rarest are the most ridiculous |
| Variants | Mutations, colors and sizes that make duplicates interesting |

Test with players: do they repeat the names and sounds to each other after one session? That is the meme test.

## Audio and content rules

- **Audio** from meme videos is usually owned by someone. Use original sounds or properly licensed audio, and keep records ([Audio and music](../07-characters-vfx-and-assets/audio-and-music.md)).
- **Age-appropriateness.** Some internet humour is crude or shocking. The game's content maturity questionnaire and Roblox's Community Standards apply; answering the questionnaire is the human's job ([Age ratings and audience](../17-policy-and-business/age-ratings-and-audience.md)).
- **Real people.** Memes built on real people (streamers, celebrities) need permission to use names and likenesses.
- **Discovery.** Titles and thumbnails must represent the game honestly; meme bait that does not match the game hurts retention and may break guidelines ([Titles and descriptions](../11-store-page-and-discovery/titles-and-descriptions.md)).

## Speed: memes are short-lived

Meme waves move faster than most format waves. A meme that is everywhere this month may be stale in two. Practical consequences:

1. **Small scope.** A meme game should reuse a proven loop (collect, steal, hatch, run) from the template and spend its time on characters and sound.
2. **Weekly characters.** New characters every week keep the meme alive inside the game even as it fades outside.
3. **Graduate the brand.** The goal is to turn borrowed attention into your own characters: by the time the meme fades, players should care about your roster.
4. **Have an exit.** If the meme fades and players leave, keep the loop and change the theme; Steal a Brainrot's loop carried on into egg and pet themes across other games in 2026 ([Trend waves](trend-waves.md)).

## The October 2026 egg wave

The studio's snapshot on 2026-10-24 showed several young egg-themed games in the breakout candidates (Break and Steal an Egg, Climb For Animals Eggs, Fake an Egg), and a tracker's weekly report for October 12-18 listed more egg games among new entries. This is meme-like spread of a theme after Steal An Egg's success, not a single meme. By the time a theme is this crowded, a new entry needs a strong twist or should skip the wave ([Hits of 2026](hits-2026.md)).

## For agents

- [ ] No third-party meme characters, names, images or sounds; original designs only, unless the human approves a license.
- [ ] Every invented name is searched for collisions.
- [ ] Meme games reuse a proven loop and plan weekly character drops.
- [ ] Content maturity questions are drafted and escalated, not answered.
