# IP and licensing

Intellectual property (IP) problems are a common way Roblox games get taken down, and repeat violations can cost accounts. This note is a practical guide, not legal advice.

## The basics

- **Copyright** protects creative works: characters, art, models, music, text, code, video.
- **Trademarks** protect brand identifiers: names, logos, slogans tied to products.
- **Ideas and mechanics are generally not protected** by copyright: "a game where you steal items from bases" is free to use. The specific expression (art, characters, names, music, code, distinctive UI) is protected.

## What not to use without a license

- Characters and names from anime, movies, cartoons, games and comics (and look-alikes designed to be recognized as them).
- Brand names and logos (cars, sports teams, fashion, fast food).
- Commercial music and sound effects you did not license.
- Real people's names and likenesses (celebrities, streamers) without permission.
- Fonts with licenses that forbid embedding or commercial use.
- Models, textures and audio copied from other games or from the internet.
- Code copied from other games (leaked or decompiled).

Roblox has licensing programs for official brand partnerships; official licensed content is marked on the platform. Without such a license, keep games original.

## Inspired by vs copying

| Usually fine | Risky or infringing |
|---|---|
| Same genre or loop as a hit | Same title, logo style and thumbnail composition |
| A creature type (dragons, robots) | A specific franchise character or a near copy |
| A general art style (stylized, anime-inspired) | Recreating a franchise's specific designs, symbols or move names |
| Original music in a genre | A track that imitates a specific copyrighted song |

Beyond legal risk, Roblox's discovery demotes non-unique games that closely resemble others, so copying fails twice.

## How takedowns work on Roblox

- Rights holders can file copyright or trademark notices (Roblox provides reporting processes and a Rights Manager tool for rights owners).
- Infringing content can be removed; repeat infringers can be suspended.
- If your content was removed by mistake, a counter-notice process exists. Filing false notices or counter-notices is illegal and against Roblox policy.

## Protecting your own game

- Keep source files and dated records of your creations (version control helps).
- Use clear ownership: the group that owns the game should also own its assets, animations and audio.
- Contractors and collaborators should agree in writing that work made for the game belongs to the game owner (or to the studio).
- If someone copies your assets or game, use Roblox's reporting processes for IP removal.

## Agents and IP

AI tools can reproduce protected characters if asked. Agents must:

- Never prompt for or produce named franchise characters, logos or brand designs.
- Create original names for everything: game titles, characters, abilities, items.
- Record provenance of every asset (see [Asset pipeline](../07-characters-vfx-and-assets/asset-pipeline.md)).

## For agents

- [ ] All characters, names, logos and music are original or licensed with records.
- [ ] No asset copied from other games or the internet.
- [ ] Contractor and collaborator ownership agreements noted.
- [ ] Titles and thumbnails do not imitate a specific existing game's branding.
