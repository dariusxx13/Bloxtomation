random.Random(20261025).

## G. Luck stacking rule and caps (Config/Economy.luau luck block)

luck = min(totalCap, (1 + Gear) x (1 + Companions) x (1 + Friends) x (1 + Sky));
Sky = min(skyCap, naturalWeather + min(paidCap, paid) + timed). Additive inside a category, multiplied across.

| Category | Sources (additive inside) | Cap | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gear | Lure +4%/level | +100% | earned |
| Companions | weather perks of equipped companions (in their weather) | +50% | earned (nests are paid random items, D2) |
| Friends | +10% per friend in the server | +50% | social |
| Sky | natural Sea Fog +100%; Lucky Tide (day 7) +25%; D6 Called Front +25%; any paid luck item | +100%; paid share at most +25% | weather, timed and paid |
| Total | product of the four | x6 |  |
