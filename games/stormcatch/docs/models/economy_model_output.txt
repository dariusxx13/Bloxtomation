random.Random(20261025).

## H. D6 Sky Bell call: effect size and cap (GDD 4.10)

A call is a natural front of the chosen type on the harbour's Callable spots (Pebble Cove values, x1) for 180 s, plus the shared bonus: Sky luck +25% for every player fishing under it. Paid luck of every kind shares one +25% cap; the Sky category is capped at +100%; the total at x6.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| fog | x2.00 | x2.00 | +0% | 29.91% -> 29.91% | +0.0% |
| gusts | x1.00 | x1.25 | +25% | 23.73% -> 28.00% | +8.8% |

Compared with play: Lure 7 alone gives x1.28; Lure 25 (Gear cap) x2.00; Gear x Companions at cap x3.00. The paid part of a call (+25%) is below Lure 7, and a called Sea Fog is never luckier than a natural one (Sky cap), so a call never gives more than free weather plus +25%.

- Coins: a natural Showers front pays +93% per minute against Clear at the harbour; a called one +105% (the shared bonus adds 12 points).
- Coins: a player at the harbour under a called Showers earns +105% per minute against Clear for 3 min, about 3.1 extra minutes of harbour income per call. The harbour pays Pebble Cove values, so for anyone past Lantern Reef (x4) a call is a collection and social moment (weather-only species, marks, journal), not income: a player at Gustwhistle earns 8x more per minute at home than under a call.
- Upper bound on coverage: 2 calls x 180 s = 50% of the 720 non-storm seconds, only on clear harbour time.
