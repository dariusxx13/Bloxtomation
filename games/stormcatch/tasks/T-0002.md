Title: Sketch the core loop and the weather twist
Owner: designer
Game / stage: stormcatch / pitch
Outcome: stay: define a loop and a weather twist that make players keep fishing and come back tomorrow, playable on a phone in the first minute.
Context: Idea from the human: "a cozy fishing game with a weather twist". The template (template/README.md) already has zones, eggs and pets, upgrades, rebirth, daily and playtime rewards, codes, shop, onboarding and analytics; prefer reusing it (for example rods as upgrades, tackle boxes as eggs, companions as pets, fishing spots as zones). Read vault/14-genre-playbooks/fishing-and-collection.md, vault/00-start-here/hit-formula.md, vault/21-hits-and-trends/anatomy-of-a-hit.md (hit-potential sheet), vault/01-first-five-minutes/first-session-blueprint.md, vault/02-core-loop/core-loop-design.md, vault/03-rarity-and-rng/collection-and-variants.md, vault/21-hits-and-trends/afk-idle-and-short-session-games.md.
Requirements:
  1. Write the one-sentence loop as "You ___ to get ___ so you can ___", concrete in Roblox terms (PITCH-02).
  2. Define "cozy" for this game in observable terms (pace, no harsh fail states, calm audio and visuals, social warmth) and what is deliberately left out.
  3. Design the weather twist so it changes how the game plays, not only how it looks: what weather does to fish, spots and catches; whether players can forecast, chase, influence or share weather; and how it creates a reason to return and to play together. Compare against at least two named fishing comparables (use the scout's market scan if it is ready; otherwise the vault's snapshot note) and say what is different (PITCH-04).
  4. Write the first 60 seconds, the goal at 10-15 minutes and the why-tomorrow hooks as player actions (PITCH-03).
  5. Name the social reason (co-play, sharing, trading or none, with argument), the session shape (long or short) and the content floor and weekly update unit.
  6. Map each mechanic to an existing template system or mark it new, with a rough size (small, medium, large).
  7. Draft the audience tier, the expected content maturity (draft only; the human answers the questionnaire) and IP notes (PITCH-07 input).
  8. Fill the hit-potential scoring sheet with a one-line justification per row.
  9. Write everything to games/stormcatch/docs/loop-sketch.md.
Out of scope: the economy sheet, prices and product catalog (design stage), the final pitch document, any code.
Constraints: House rules in CLAUDE.md. Original names and themes only; no franchise or other games' names, characters or art. Do not answer policy questions; mark them as questions for the human. Numbers that are not yet modelled are labelled as targets, not facts.
Acceptance criteria:
  - The loop sentence is concrete and playable (PITCH-02)
  - First 60 s, 10-15 min goal and why-tomorrow are player actions (PITCH-03)
  - The twist names at least two comparables and states how play differs (PITCH-04)
  - Template reuse map and content floor are present
  - Hit-potential sheet is filled with justifications
Evidence to return: path to loop-sketch.md and a five-line summary (loop, twist, first goal, social reason, biggest risk).
Dependencies: none (read the scout's market scan if it exists when you start)
Hand off to: scout (pitch doc), who needs the loop, twist, first-session timeline and risks.
