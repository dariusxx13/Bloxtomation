### The two decisions
- **Auto Reel pause: adopted** (GDD §4.12 rule 8). Auto Reel pauses after 10 minutes in which the only input was Cast; any other input resumes it at no cost, with a "tap to resume" toast. Reasons:
  - It closes the AFK farm the sheet found: about 29 minutes of active income per unattended hour, against 0.5 minute for a Tide Pot hour.
  - It keeps Auto Reel's rule 3 ("Not AFK") true.
  - It costs a present player nothing.
  - How the server detects other input is for the Engineer and the Security Reviewer. `AutoReelPaused` is added to analytics (§10).
- **Easier reeling in Gusts: keep more of the sway** (GDD §4.12). The sway goes to ±30° instead of ±20°, because Gusts is the one weather presented as a skill challenge, and a skilled player would gain +14% there just by flipping the switch. The 1.5x arc still makes Gusts much easier for players who need help. Sea Fog keeps the full assist: its difficulty is perceptual, and the assist leaves that in place. The sheet says the economy is fine either way, and ±30° only lowers its modelled Gusts gain.
