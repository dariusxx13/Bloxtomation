## Summary
Outcome served: **stay**. I wrote the Reel the Storm economy sheet (`games/stormcatch/docs/economy-sheet.md`) and its reproducible model, `games/stormcatch/docs/models/economy_model.py`; the stored output is in `games/stormcatch/docs/models/economy_model_output.txt`. The model is a deterministic expected-value pacing simulation for three player types over the 15 storm join minutes, plus seeded Monte Carlo odds checks. It covers catch values, bite waits per weather, the Rod, Lure, Boots and Pal Pouch curves, bridge prices, New Tide, the Great Storm and Stormborn, rewards and Tide Pots, drop tables per island and weather with time-to-see per tier, nests with hard pity, one capped luck stacking rule that includes the D6 Sky Bell bonus, and a paste-ready prototype subset. The casual free player reaches the first bridge at 10.8 min (range 10.3-11.1). The GDD's §4.10 call timing is used as given. Every place where the sheet changes a GDD "economy sheet (T-0008)" value is listed under Evidence for the Designer to sync. No code, config or other role's document was edited, and nothing was committed.
