## Summary
I sketched the Stormcatch core loop and the weather twist in `games/stormcatch/docs/loop-sketch.md`. Outcome served: **stay**, with **return** second. The scout's market scan found that weather changing spawn odds, adding mutations, or being summoned or bought is already standard in the fishing leaders. I reworked the twist so those parts are only the familiar layer. The twist is now "each weather is a different way to fish, and it moves": rain gives quick, forgiving reels, fog is fishing by feel, gusts carry the cast and sway the reel target, and Great Storm giants are landed fastest when a crowd pulls together. Fronts drift between islands on a short in-game forecast, and storms are safe. All four parts sit in the scan's "not found" column. I cut weather summoning (the Sky Bell) from launch because it is standard and would make the shared forecast untrue. No code was changed.
