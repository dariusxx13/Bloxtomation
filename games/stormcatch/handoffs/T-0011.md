## Summary
I wrote the world plan for Reel the Storm in `games/stormcatch/docs/world-plan.md`. Outcome served: **stay**. The map is a compact hub and three spokes around a lagoon. Pebble Cove (Driftlight Harbor and the Open Pier) is the hub, and short bridges lead to Lantern Reef, Gustwhistle Bluffs and Thunderhead Point, with a ferry across the lagoon. Every coordinate lives in one stdlib script, `games/stormcatch/docs/models/world_layout.py`. It computes walking paths at the template's base walk speed of 16 studs/s, checks the cozy rules, cast ranges, weather cells, the sheltered Dock Ripple, the `Callable` harbour spots, ferry clearance and first-frame sightlines, and draws the SVG and ASCII maps. Its run ends with "All checks pass". After the Director's note, the plan agrees with the accepted GDD: front routes skip the Dock Ripple, spot ids are unchanged, the `Callable` harbour spots are marked, and the prototype subset follows GDD §13 and `specs/prototype.md`. No Studio work, lease, asset or paid service was used, and no code was changed.
