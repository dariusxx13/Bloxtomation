- Sky Bell price reasoning (catalog §3.1, [E]):
  - **Parity with the Coin Pouch.** At the GDD's target of buyer plus 3 others under a call, at 3.1 min each (economy sheet §13), a call is 12.4 player-minutes of harbour income for 49 Robux: 0.25 min per Robux, against the Pouch's 0.31.
  - **Not 25.** At 25 a call would be 1.6x the Pouch's value, the cheapest way to buy income, and would fill the 2-calls-per-cycle supply.
  - **Not 99.** The buyer's own share is 0.06 min per Robux, so a call is a gift, not a deal.
  - **One price for all three weathers.** The weather choice is a preference, not a power tier.
  - **Spend ceiling.** At most 196 Robux an hour per buyer, if the Designer adopts one call per buyer per cycle.
