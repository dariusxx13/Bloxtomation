### Changes adopted, and the one decision

- **Economy sheet changes (sheet §15): all adopted, none argued against.**
  - Tide Pots at 0.5 min of income per pot-hour, 8 fish (G1): adopted. It is below the vault's usual 10-50% offline band, but the vault's binding checklist rule is that passive income stays below half for active players. At 25%, pots would be 91% of a typical day's coins. GDD §4.8 now says this.
  - Stormborn 4.0% with pity at 60 (G2), helper share 10% (G3), best island and half value (G4), +25% Sky luck (G5), prototype values and Hushgill Rare (G6), Thunderhead without New Tide (G9), and the luck rule and caps: all adopted.
  - **Watch item on G3, not a disagreement.** The sheet's reason for 10% ("at 25% a new player's first storm paid more than 3 minutes of fishing") seems to come from the earlier Stormborn value factor 1.5 (sheet §16 row 26). At the final 0.5, a help pays about what the helper would catch at the eye in the same 10-12 s; in the prototype, where Rumblefin is the only giant, it pays more. The reason to help is the Witnessed stamp and the crowd. That is acceptable, and the friend test (loop test 4) checks whether players still help. I added it to the GDD §12 prototype questions.
- **World plan changes: all adopted.** Ferry stops (GDD §4.5 reworded; the 60 s reach holds on foot). Dock Ripple position (§3.1, brief req. 2). Rock pools on Pebble Point (§4.1). Spot attributes `SpotKind`, `Sheltered`, `Callable`, `StormEye`, `CellId`, `CastPoint` and tags `WeatherCell`, `StormHome`, `FogLantern`, `SkyBell` (§4.1, §9.4, brief req. 2). Tap-to-walk to `CastPoint` (§4.1, brief req. 3.1 and a new acceptance criterion). Ferry launch scope (world plan question 5): in, size S, and the first item cut if the build stage runs late.
- **First-frame shower: the timetable stays (GDD §3.1, "First frame").** The prototype timetable gives a shower in 44.4% of first frames, fog 22.2%, the storm 20.0% and clear water 13.3%, so some weather appears in 86.7%; the Thunderhead storm cloud is in 100%. More harbour Showers would raise twist test B's chance baseline: every extra 100 s adds 4.6 points, and a shower in two-thirds of first frames takes coverage from 27.8% to 37.0%. That narrows the margin to the 45% pass bar from 17 points to 8. The alternative is to drop Sea Fog, which twist test A needs. It would also reopen the sheet's §14 numbers and the world plan's §12 paths. The prototype checks the decision with first-frame captures (brief Evidence and Hand off to); the fallback lever is visual, not the schedule.
- **Storm fishing at the eye only.** The GDD said every spot used the storm ring, with Charged flash taps. The economy model treats storm time away from the eye as Clear (`economy_model.py` line 612), and the sheet's eye numbers (1.61x Clear) depend on that. I aligned the GDD and the brief with the model rather than leave the two documents disagreeing. This also pulls players to the pier (pillar 3).
