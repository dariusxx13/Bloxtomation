### Decisions and their reasons

- **W1 and W2: one approach, natural harbour visits.** Rejected alternative: calls that withhold marks or records natural weather does not give. Reasons:
  1. A call stays "exactly like a natural front of its type", a rule a child can see. Withholding Misty from a Pebble Minnow but not from a Hushgill would be invisible, and the catalog's odds lists would need exceptions.
  2. The hub gains all three ways of fishing (pillar 2). The prototype already runs Sea Fog over the harbour.
  3. Cost: two routes and four schedule invariants, with no new system.
- **Visits fit the existing guarantees.** Each visit sits on a harbour front spot just before Showers arrives there, so the onboarding rain wait, the Sky Bell's 180 s window and every Rainbow are unchanged; the script above checks all three.
- **Per-buyer limit:** counted by the cycle a call starts in and stored in the profile, so it survives a server change. At most 4 calls an hour for one buyer, which matches catalog [E].
- **Name switch on by default:** the vault's rule for server-wide purchases is "Show who bought it" (`vault/09-monetization/product-catalog.md`), and the public thank-you is the buyer's only reward under D6. The switch lets a child ring quietly.
- **Easier reeling:** free, off by default and available from the first session. It scales every weather's ring rather than flattening it, so the twist still reads, and it takes no reward away. Hold-to-pull counts as one tap a second, so it gives no extra pull. It stays out of the prototype, so twist test A and the 30-second test measure the designed ring.
- **Auto Reel:** the catalog's §3.3 rules, recorded as design rules; the numbers (70% landed, x2 reel time) stay cited to the catalog until the Economist confirms them.
